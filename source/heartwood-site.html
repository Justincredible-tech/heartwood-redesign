About Heartwood

Roots

Heartwood is a regional network that protects forests and supports community activism in the eastern United States through education, advocacy, and citizen empowerment.

Heartwood was founded in 1991, when concerned citizens from several midwestern states each defending their national forest from logging, mining, roads and ruin, met and began to work together to protect the heartland hardwood forest.

From the Ozarks to the Appalachians, the Ohio River basin and the Cumberland Plateau, the Heartwood region encompasses tremendous natural beauty and ecological diversity.
