What you will find us working on

Much of the eastern hardwood forest survives only as fragments of public land. Each symbol below is a threat our member groups track, and a campaign you can join.

Keeping watch on National Forest land and acting when management does harm.

Forest, Climate and Carbon

Oil and Gas Drilling

Tracking the industry's expansion onto public land.

Biomass Incineration

Opposing plants that burn forests for power.

Mountaintop Removal

Standing with communities working to end mountaintop removal coal mining.

Sustainable Communities

Building places that depend less on extraction and exploitation.
Forest Watch

Somebody has to read the timber sale notices

No commercial logging on public lands.

Heartwood was among the first organizations to say it publicly. Forest Watch is how we back it up: members watch what the Forest Service proposes, tell their neighbors, and take the agency to court when it breaks its own rules.
