## Common brief

Use case: logo-brand. Generate ONE new standalone flat icon of a hardwood tree fused with the CAPITAL LETTER "T". The attached image is a STRUCTURAL REFERENCE for the desired integration of typography, foliage and negative space, not an edit target: study the recognizable serif capital T at the lower center, the explicit short straight horizontal crossbar below the foliage, and especially the elegant curved cutouts under the crown and the branching light-colored negative space running up through the crown. Create an original variant of this approach. The black foliage MUST curve downward into the T's horizontal bar through a narrow central neck; curved background-colored apertures on the left and right of that neck reveal the crossbar clearly. Keep the crossbar visibly horizontal with short left and right arms and a substantial straight centered vertical stem beneath; the stem has balanced terminals, never a lowercase hook. The tree crown is broad deciduous hardwood foliage, oak/maple inspired. Light-colored negative-space branches are carved THROUGH the dark crown and converge toward the bar, giving a dual reading of abstract tree and clearly capital T. Be a refined typographic abstraction with restrained shapes. One solid black foreground color on a uniform pale neutral gray background (#F5F5F5), pure flat vector-like silhouette, crisp smooth edges. No gradients, texture, shadows, outlines, 3D, wordmark, labels, borders, or extra symbols. One icon centered on a square canvas, generous margins, total mark about 65 percent of canvas height.
