## Common brief

Use case: logo-brand. Generate ONE flat icon logo: a CAPITAL uppercase letter "T" reimagined as a mature HARDWOOD TREE. Use the five attached images ONLY as visual style references for black filled silhouettes, broad deciduous crowns, simple sturdy trunks, and sparse negative-space branch forks. Create a NEW distinct letterform, do not reproduce a reference. The tree and uppercase T are the SAME integrated silhouette: a wide horizontal canopy serves as the top crossbar; a straight centered substantial trunk forms the vertical stem below. The visual skeleton must read as CAPITAL T at small sizes. No trunk or shoot above the canopy, no lowercase curved foot. Canopy is broad and relatively shallow, about 2.5 to 3 times the width of trunk, with tree-specific outer contour and a mostly horizontal underside; tall exposed trunk below. One solid pure black fill on a uniform light neutral gray background (#F5F5F5). Crisp vector-like flat icon, no texture, gradients, shadows, shading, 3D, outlines, text, border, scenery, watermark, or other icons. Center ONE icon on a square canvas with ample margin.
